Space Birthday Privacy Policy

Birth dates and profiles are stored on your device. Space Birthday does not require an account.

1. Operator and contact

Dariusz Korolczuk operates Space Birthday and is the controller of personal data received when providing user support. For app and privacy enquiries, email kontakt@dariuszkorolczuk.com.

2. Data on your device

The app stores your selected birth date and profiles you add: a name, birth date, optional email address, profile identifier, creation date, and calendar and reminder preferences. The profile email address is optional reference information. The app does not send messages or invitations to it.

Ages and upcoming birthdays are calculated on your device. These details are not sent to the operator's server. The app has no user accounts, developer-operated cloud profile sync, advertising or advertising trackers. App data may be included in device backups depending on your Apple settings. The operator cannot access those backups.

3. Calendar and reminders

When you request it and grant permission, the app can add events to your system calendar. Event titles contain the planet name and calculated age. Notes explain that the result is an estimate based on an average orbital period and that Space Birthday created the event; they do not contain the original birth date. This feature uses permission to add events and does not need to read your calendar.

Your selected calendar may sync events through iCloud or another calendar account provider according to its policies and your settings. Deleting a profile or the app does not remove events already added. You can remove them in Calendar. Reminders are scheduled locally after notification permission is granted. Notification text may appear on your lock screen depending on iOS settings.

4. Premium purchases

Premium is an optional one-time in-app purchase with no automatic renewal. Apple handles payment and purchase restoration. Space Birthday checks product and transaction entitlement information to unlock features. The operator does not receive your card number or payment account details. App Store processing is also covered by Apple's privacy policy.

5. NASA and other external links

Daily Space provides a link to the official Astronomy Picture of the Day (APOD) website. The app does not automatically download APOD images or descriptions. A connection to the external website is made only when you open its link in your browser. Saved names, birth dates and email addresses are not included in that link.

Websites you visit and their providers may receive your IP address, browser details and other connection information, and may use cookies under their own policies. NASA explains its practices in its privacy policy. Other linked websites may have different policies. Space Birthday is not a NASA app and is not sponsored by NASA.

6. Support correspondence and website

If you email us, we receive your sender address, message, attachments and information needed to deliver and answer it. Please omit birth dates, other people's email addresses and screenshots containing their information unless needed to resolve your request. A problem description, device model and iOS version are usually sufficient.

Email sent to our contact address is received by Amazon Simple Email Service in eu-west-1, temporarily stored in private Amazon S3 storage and forwarded to the operator's Gmail mailbox provided by Google. Raw messages in S3 are subject to a 30-day deletion lifecycle. Messages in Gmail are retained for as long as needed to handle your enquiry, or longer when needed to meet a legal obligation or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. They are deleted manually.

Correspondence may be processed outside the European Economic Area. See AWS data protection information and Google's transfer framework information for their transfer safeguards. These support and privacy pages contain no analytics or advertising scripts. The hosting provider receives connection data needed to serve the page.

7. Purposes, legal bases and your rights

Local data supports the features you choose: calculations, profiles, calendar events and reminders. The operator does not receive a copy. We process correspondence to answer questions and provide support, based on our legitimate interest in assisting users (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We also process data-protection requests to meet legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).

For data received by the operator, you may request access, correction, deletion or restriction and, where applicable, portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Rights depend on the circumstances. Contact us at the address above. You can lodge a complaint with the Polish Personal Data Protection Office or your relevant supervisory authority. We do not make decisions with legal effects through automated profiling.

8. Deletion and permissions

You can edit or delete a profile in its settings. Local app data remains until you remove a profile or delete the app and its data. Offloading the app while retaining its data is different from deleting it. You manage device backups through Apple settings. There is no Space Birthday account to delete separately.

You can change calendar and notification permissions in iOS Settings. Deleting the app does not delete your Apple purchase history or events exported to Calendar. Email us for requests about support correspondence.
