Math Birthday Privacy Policy

Math Birthday calculates your milestones on your device. The app works without an account.

Who operates Math Birthday?

Dariusz Korolczuk operates Math Birthday and handles personal data received through support correspondence. For privacy questions, email kontakt@dariuszkorolczuk.com.

Profiles and settings

The app saves the birth date and, if provided, the birth time you use for calculations. A profile you add can also contain a name and an optional email address for your reference. The app stores a profile identifier, creation date, selected milestones and reminder settings to manage the profile.

The app stores and uses these details on your device, without sending them to the developer. The app operates without ads, analytics or tracking. App data may be included in backups according to your Apple settings. The developer has no access to those backups.

Calendar, reminders and sharing

When you choose to add a milestone and grant calendar access, the app adds it to your system calendar. An event can include the milestone, its date and the birth date used in the calculation. Your calendar can sync through iCloud or another calendar provider according to your settings.

If you enable reminders and grant permission, the app schedules them on the device. Notification previews can show the milestone on your Lock Screen, depending on your settings. You can change calendar and notification permissions in Settings.

When you use the system share sheet, you choose the app or recipient that receives your milestone results. Information shared this way is handled by the destination you choose.

Premium purchases

Premium is an optional one-time purchase. Apple processes the payment and provides purchase restoration through the App Store. Math Birthday uses Apple’s product and verified purchase information to unlock Premium. The developer does not receive your payment card or Apple Account credentials. See Apple’s privacy policy for its handling of purchase data.

Support and website visits

If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments so we can respond. A description of the issue and your device model and system version are usually enough. Please leave out names, birth dates and other private profile details unless they are needed to resolve your request.

Our support address receives mail through Amazon Simple Email Service and forwards it to the operator’s Gmail inbox. Amazon S3 temporarily stores incoming messages with a 30-day expiration policy. Correspondence in the inbox is kept for as long as needed to respond and resolve the issue, or longer when a legal obligation or dispute requires it. Providers may process correspondence outside the European Economic Area. See AWS data protection information and Google’s transfer framework information.

The hosting provider receives connection information such as your IP address to serve these pages. The pages use no advertising or analytics scripts.

Deleting information and managing permissions

You can edit or delete a saved profile in its settings. To remove all local app data, delete the app and its data from your device. You can manage backups separately in Apple settings. Events you have already exported remain in your calendar until you delete them there. Apple manages your purchase history.

For questions about support correspondence or requests to access, correct or delete personal data the developer holds, contact kontakt@dariuszkorolczuk.com.
