Your age on other planets

Choose a birth date to see your age in other planets' years and the dates of your upcoming birthdays. Add profiles for people you know and switch between them. Calculations use average orbital periods and are educational approximations. Pluto is a dwarf planet.

Free features and Premium

Earth, Venus and Mars are available for free. An optional Premium purchase unlocks the remaining planets and Pluto, calendar events for the next five years and local reminders. This is a one-time purchase with no subscription or automatic renewal. The App Store displays the price for your region before you confirm payment.

After reinstalling, open Premium and choose “Restore Purchases” using the same Apple Account used to buy it. If the offer does not load, check your internet connection and try again. Apple handles refund requests at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Calendar and reminders

Allow calendar additions and notifications when you want to use those features. You can change permissions in iPhone Settings. Notification delivery also depends on Focus and iOS settings. The app schedules reminders for the next birthday on each planet. To refresh the schedule, open the app and tap “Refresh reminders”. Opening the app alone does not refresh reminders. When capacity is limited, the nearest birthdays are scheduled first.

Adding a series covers birthdays within the next five years. Events previously saved by the app are skipped when adding them again. Events already added to Calendar remain after you delete a profile or the app. Remove them separately in Calendar. Event sync depends on your calendar account.

Profiles and privacy

Use a profile's settings to edit its name, birth date and optional email, or delete the profile. These details stay on your device; the app does not send invitations to those email addresses. No account is required. Read the privacy policy for details.

Daily Space

The link in Daily Space opens the official Astronomy Picture of the Day website in your browser and requires internet access. Content and availability depend on the external service. Space Birthday is not a NASA product.

Reporting a problem

Include your app version, iOS version, device model and the steps that led to the problem. Do not send your Apple Account password or payment details. Hide private profile information before sending screenshots.
