Find your milestones

Choose a birth date and tap Calculate. Use the results filters to see upcoming or reached milestones in days, hours, minutes or seconds. One day is counted as 24 hours from the time of birth. A birth time entered to the minute makes second-level results approximate. Dates are also approximate when the birth time is unknown.

Free access and Premium

The free version shows every reached milestone and the next three upcoming milestones across all time units. Filtering by unit narrows this list. Premium unlocks the full upcoming timeline and lets you select milestones for a saved profile. It is a one-time purchase. The App Store shows the price for your region before payment.

To restore access after reinstalling or changing devices, open Premium and tap Restore Purchases while using the Apple Account you originally used to buy it. If the offer fails to load, check your internet connection and try again. Apple handles refund requests through Report a Problem.

Calendar, reminders and sharing

In your results, expand Calendar, Reminders & Sharing. Allow Calendar access when you choose to add events. Exported events can sync through your selected calendar account. Delete an exported event in Calendar if you want to remove it.

To use Premium reminders, provide a birth time and allow notifications. Focus and notification settings can affect delivery. Use the share action to send the next milestones to a destination you select.

Manage a profile

Choose a saved person and calculate their milestones, then tap the gear in the results. Choose Edit Person to make changes or Delete Person to remove the local profile. The optional email is for your reference. For details, read the privacy policy.

Report a problem

Include the device model, system version, app version and steps that led to the problem. Remove private profile details from screenshots before sending them.
